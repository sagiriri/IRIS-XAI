Ground-Truth Part Overlap
FunnyBirds only

Of each explanation's most important pixels, what fraction actually land on a real bird part (beak/eye/foot/wing) vs. background clutter? Unlike every other metric on this page, this checks against real ground truth rather than the explanation's own internal consistency — computed via funnybirds_ground_truth.py using the dataset's canonical part-map colors.

Faithfulness — Deletion vs. Insertion AUC
XAI Module

Deletion AUC: lower is better (confidence should crash fast when top-ranked pixels are removed). Insertion AUC: higher is better (confidence should climb fast when they're revealed). Grouped by model, one pair of bars per XAI method.

Stability
XAI Module

Cosine similarity of the explanation under small input noise — higher means the explanation doesn't flip around for near-identical inputs.

Complexity (Entropy)
XAI Module

Normalized Shannon entropy of the explanation map — lower means the map concentrates on fewer regions, which is easier for a human to read.
